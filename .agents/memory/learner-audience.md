name: Learner audience and tutor
description: Age range, study-guide character direction, and Arabic address for the YAEL app.
---

The app is for students aged 17–24. Use a young adult peer tutor for study guidance instead of a cat.

**Why:** The user stated the age range and explicitly said he does not like the teacher being a cat. A modern young human tutor was chosen to suit that audience.

**How to apply:** Keep the guide relatable to late teens and university-age learners, not styled for small children. Preserve customizable profile avatars as a separate feature.

Address the learner in masculine Arabic, not feminine.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested the male version of learner-facing language.

**How to apply:** Use masculine forms in instructional, feedback, completion, and onboarding copy.
