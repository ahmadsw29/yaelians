---
name: Learner audience and tutor
description: Age range, study-guide character direction, and Arabic address for the YAEL app.
---

The app is for students aged 17–24. The primary guide should be an imaginary pet that looks hyped and cute, with supporting characters presenting questions beside the question text, not at the head of the progress bar.

**Why:** The user rejected Rami's appearance and asked for an unreal, hyped, cute pet and a playful learning experience like Duolingo. He is open to keeping Rami as a supporting character, but does not want a character on the progress line.

**How to apply:** Use original expressive creatures and question-presenter characters, not a cat or a copy of Duolingo's owl. Keep the experience relatable to late teens and university-age learners. Preserve customizable profile avatars as a separate feature.

Address the learner in masculine Arabic, not feminine.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested the male version of learner-facing language.

**How to apply:** Use masculine forms in instructional, feedback, completion, and onboarding copy.

Keep answer feedback near the next/back controls visual: a character and a check/cross, without extra praise or encouragement sentences. A wrong-answer correction may still show the actual correct answer.

**Why:** The user previously asked to remove the “إجابة رائعة” and other encouragement messages and leave the character beside the correctness symbol.

**How to apply:** Do not reintroduce congratulatory or consoling lines when restyling practice navigation.

Use the same Arabic typeface in practice questions, answers, and navigation as on the home page.

**Why:** The user disliked the practice-screen Arabic and explicitly requested the home-page font.

**How to apply:** Keep Arabic typography shared instead of giving question bubbles and buttons separate font choices.

Vocabulary practice should show progress, the vocabulary category, the word and example, all four answers, and Next together without scrolling at normal text size. Keep Next near the bottom navigation, not far above it.

**Why:** The user explicitly corrected an oversized bottom gap and said he wants everything visible on one page without scrolling.

**How to apply:** Use a compact question layout and a persistent header; do not solve footer overlap by reserving excessive empty space. Preserve access to content when text is enlarged.
