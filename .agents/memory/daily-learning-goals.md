---
name: Daily learning goals
description: Learner-chosen numerical daily goals, meaningful counting, and separation from plan limits and mastery.
---
Ask learners to choose numerical daily targets for words, questions in each section, and completed models (نماذج), and show them in “مسارك اليوم”.
**Why:** The user requested goal-setting when opening the app and chose separate words/questions goals, explicitly adding نماذج.
**How to apply:** Prompt once per device-local day when no targets are selected; allow editing or postponing. Never interrupt an active timed practice to ask again.

Daily targets are planning aids, not earned mastery, XP, or permission to bypass a plan allowance.
**Why:** A self-chosen number does not demonstrate learning or grant extra access; flashcard review and independent quiz mastery already have distinct meanings in this app.
**How to apply:** Count distinct reviewed words across vocabulary and flashcards, answered questions by section, and fully answered models with submitted essays. Changing a target must retain actual activity, not manufacture completions. A zero target means “not today”.

Goals currently use the device-local day and on-device account-scoped storage.
**Why:** This keeps the planner usable alongside offline learning without changing authoritative server quotas or XP. Cross-device goal synchronization is separate work.
**How to apply:** Keep the device-only disclosure honest, preserve damaged data rather than silently resetting it, and do not confuse local goal rollover with the server's free-usage reset.
