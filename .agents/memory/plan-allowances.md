The current monetization direction is an installable web app for iPhone and Android, with website payments at ₪48.90 per month and payouts in Israel. Active subscribers get all courses and unlimited practice.

**Why:** The user chose an installable web app and monthly billing after asking to require website payment before making the app available to install. This supersedes the intervening request to make everything free.

**How to apply:** Link access to a server-verified subscription and the signed-in learner, not possession of an installation/download link. Keep billing disabled until a compatible provider is connected and checkout is verified. Do not silently change shekels to another currency or treat RevenueCat's native-store connection as web checkout. Earlier trial allowances below describe the existing app, not authorization to bypass paid website access.

Call the free tier “تجربة مجانية” everywhere in learner-facing copy.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked to replace “خطة مجانية” with “تجربة مجانية” in every location.

**How to apply:** This is a naming change, not permission to add an expiry, automatic conversion, or charges; retain the existing allowance rules.

Free accounts get one five-minute vocabulary round per day, one timed exam section per day, and access to the single free exam model. Premium has unlimited sessions and courses.

**Why:** The user corrected the extra-practice loophole and explicitly specified “One 5 mins round per day, with the words in the practice that do not repeat everytime.”

**How to apply:** Free users must not receive untracked additional rounds through a “واصل التدريب” button after their allowance is used. Prioritize unseen vocabulary over unnecessary repeats of correct answers, while allowing mistakes to return sooner for learning. Do not reintroduce the older unlimited-untracked-practice behavior for free accounts.

The premium price requested by the user is ₪48.90 per month, not ₪50.

**Why:** The user explicitly changed the price from 50 to 48.90.

**How to apply:** Keep all price copy and eventual store products consistent with that amount; do not treat a complimentary testing grant as a paid subscription.
